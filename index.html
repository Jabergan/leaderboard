Why this exists

The composite score is the product.

Most AI leaderboards take 5–10 benchmarks, weight them by a secret formula, and produce one number. That number is useful for press releases and placement on a list. It is not useful for understanding what a model actually does well.

This page shows every benchmark in its own column. Sort by the one that matters for your use case. If you want a composite, you can build one — weight GPQA heavily if you're a researcher, HumanEval if you're shipping code, MMLU if you just want general knowledge. Your weights, your model.

Sources: MMLU reported by model providers. MATH / HumanEval from official evals. GPQA from papers. Agentic scores from AA Intelligence Index v4.3 (Elo-500)/2000. Pricing from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta public API docs. Speed from provider benchmarks.
