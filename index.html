Why this exists

The composite score is the product.

Most AI leaderboards take 5–10 benchmarks, weight them by a secret formula, and produce one number. That number is useful for press releases. It is not useful for understanding what a model actually does well.

This page shows every benchmark in its own column. Sort by the one that matters for your use case. If you're shipping code, sort by HumanEval. Researcher? Sort GPQA. Just want general knowledge? MMLU. Your weights, your model.
